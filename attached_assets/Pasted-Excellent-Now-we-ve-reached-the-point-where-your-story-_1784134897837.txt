Why This Scene Is Critical

This is your “Rocky training montage” scene.

The audience must see that the app wasn’t magically created.

They need to feel:

* confusion
* mistakes
* learning
* frustration
* persistence

Only then will the launch and 500-download scenes feel earned.

🎬 Next Scene (Scene 8 — The Collapse) should be even stronger: a major setback where the project nearly dies—bugs, lost work, exhaustion, or the moment the founders consider abandoning the app entirely. That’s often the emotional low point before the final rise.
