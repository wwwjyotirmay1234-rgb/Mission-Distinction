If you’re rebuilding from scratch, don’t write Scene 1 as an image prompt. Write it as a movie screenplay + shot list.

SCENE 1 — THE STRUGGLE

Duration: 45–60 seconds

⸻

Opening Screen

Black Screen

Sound:

* Heavy rain
* Distant thunder
* Clock ticking

Narrator:

“Every year, thousands of students enter medical college with a dream.”

Pause.

“A dream to become a doctor.”

Fade in.

⸻

SHOT 1 — THE HOSTEL

Duration: 4 seconds

Visual:

A medical college hostel building at 2:17 AM.

Heavy rain falls.

Most rooms are dark.

Only one room is lit.

Camera slowly moves toward that window.

Narrator:

“But dreams meet reality.”

⸻

SHOT 2 — THE ROOM

Duration: 5 seconds

Visual:

Camera passes through the rain-covered window.

Inside:

* Gray’s Anatomy
* Guyton Physiology
* Biochemistry books
* Crumpled notes
* Empty coffee mug
* Sticky notes on wall

Desk lamp is the only light source.

A young MBBS student sits silently.

⸻

SHOT 3 — THE STUDENT

Duration: 5 seconds

Visual:

Side profile.

Curly black hair.

Dark hoodie.

Tired eyes.

Unshaven face.

He stares at notes.

Trying to focus.

Narrator:

“The syllabus seemed endless.”

⸻

SHOT 4 — THE BOOKS

Duration: 4 seconds

Visual:

Close-ups.

Pages flipping.

Anatomy diagrams.

Physiology notes.

Biochemistry pathways.

Piles of unfinished work.

Camera slowly pans.

Narrator:

“The books kept growing.”

⸻

SHOT 5 — THE EYES

Duration: 4 seconds

Visual:

Extreme close-up.

Eyes red.

Dark circles visible.

Rain reflections in pupils.

Blinking slowly.

Narrator:

“The pressure kept growing.”

⸻

SHOT 6 — THE SYLLABUS

Duration: 5 seconds

Visual:

Over-the-shoulder shot.

Notebook open.

Long checklist.

Most topics unfinished.

Only a few tick marks.

Student freezes.

Heart sinks.

⸻

SHOT 7 — THE PEN

Duration: 3 seconds

Visual:

Student tries writing.

Stops.

Hand trembles.

Pen slips.

Falls onto notebook.

Sound:

Small pen drop.

Silence.

⸻

SHOT 8 — THE DOUBT

Duration: 6 seconds

Visual:

Student leans back.

Looks at ceiling.

Rain outside.

Desk lamp flickering softly.

Expression changes:

Determination → Frustration → Doubt.

Narrator:

“And sometimes…”

Pause.

“He wondered if he was already falling behind.”

⸻

SHOT 9 — THE WORLD MOVES ON

Duration: 5 seconds

Visual:

Phone screen.

Class group messages.

Friends discussing preparation.

Messages arriving rapidly.

Student silently watches.

Feels left behind.

⸻

SHOT 10 — THE LOWEST POINT

Duration: 6 seconds

Visual:

Wide shot.

Student appears small.

Books appear overwhelming.

Room feels huge.

Silence.

Narrator:

“He wasn’t the only one.”

Pause.

“Across Odisha…”

Pause.

“Hundreds of students felt exactly the same.”

⸻

FINAL SHOT

Duration: 5 seconds

Visual:

Close-up.

Student slowly looks up.

A thought begins.

Not hope yet.

Just an idea.

Rain continues.

Fade to black.

⸻

Text Appears

“What if the problem wasn’t the students…”

Pause.

“What if the problem was the lack of guidance?”

Fade out.

⸻

Transition To Scene 2

Sound:

Rain fades.

Single piano note.

Black screen.

Text:

“One Idea Changed Everything.”
