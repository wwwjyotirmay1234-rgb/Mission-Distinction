I can see what’s happening from your screenshots.

Replit is treating your screenplay like a web page with text overlays, not like a cinematic movie. That’s why you get:

* Large paragraphs on screen
* Static images
* Minimal character movement
* No emotional storytelling
* Looks like a presentation instead of a film

The issue is not the story. The issue is the prompt structure.

Instead of giving Replit narration like:

“Books scattered everywhere. Highlighters dried out. Notes incomplete…”

give it director instructions.

Replace this:

Books scattered everywhere. Highlighters dried out. Notes incomplete. The syllabus feels endless…

With this:

Close-up shot of an exhausted MBBS student studying alone at 2:17 AM in a hostel room. Camera slowly pushes toward his face. Rain hits the window. He rubs his eyes and looks at a thick anatomy book. Desk lamp creates warm light while the rest of the room remains dark. No text on screen. No narration visible.

Create a cinematic animated short film. DO NOT display paragraphs, subtitles, narration text, captions, quotes, scene descriptions, or screenplay text on screen.
