ANIMATION RULES FOR REPLIT

Tell Replit:

Use cinematic camera movements only. Slow push-ins, slow pans, slow orbit shots, rack focus, foreground blur, realistic blinking, subtle breathing, moving rain, moving curtains, moving notebook pages. No fast cuts. No cartoon animation. No bouncing objects. No exaggerated reactions. Every shot should feel like a scene from a Hollywood drama film.

This will give Replit much clearer direction than a simple story prompt and greatly improve the chances of getting a cinematic result.
