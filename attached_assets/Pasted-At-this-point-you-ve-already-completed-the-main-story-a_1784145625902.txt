At this point, you’ve already completed the main story arc.

If you want to continue beyond the ending, don’t call it “Next Scene.”

Call it:

🎬 AFTER THE CREDITS

“The First Anniversary”

This scene is inspired by how Hollywood films show the consequences of a mission after success.

For Mission Distinction, this would be one year after launch.

⸻
