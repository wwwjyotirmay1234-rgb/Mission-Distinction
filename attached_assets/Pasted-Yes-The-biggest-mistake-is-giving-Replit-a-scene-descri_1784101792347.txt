Yes. The biggest mistake is giving Replit a scene description instead of a movie direction sheet.

For Scene 1, give Replit this entire specification.

SCENE 1 — THE STRUGGLE

Duration

45–60 seconds

Visual Style (Mandatory)

Create a cinematic 3D animated film sequence.

Unreal Engine 5 quality.

Pixar-level character realism.

Arcane-inspired lighting.

Realistic Indian MBBS student.

Highly expressive facial animation.

Volumetric lighting.

Cinematic depth of field.

Dynamic camera movement.

Realistic cloth simulation.

Realistic rain effects.

Emotional storytelling.

Hollywood Oscar-winning animation quality.

NOT flat vector art.

NOT infographic style.

NOT educational cartoon.

NOT stick figures.

NOT low-poly characters.

⸻

CHARACTER DESIGN

Main Character:

* Male
* Indian
* Age 20
* MBBS student
* Curly black hair
* Slight beard
* No glasses
* Slim build
* Wearing dark hoodie
* Sleep deprived appearance
* Intelligent face
* Expressive eyes

Keep this exact appearance throughout the movie.

⸻

MBBS hostel room.

Details:

* Gray’s Anatomy book
* Guyton Physiology
* Biochemistry textbook
* Scattered notes
* Highlighters
* Coffee mug
* Study lamp
* Rain outside window
* Wall calendar
* Medical diagrams pinned to wall

Mood:

Lonely.

Silent.

Overwhelmed.

Final Narration

Use voice-over:

“Every year, thousands of MBBS students begin their journey with dreams.”

“But dreams alone do not make the syllabus smaller.”

“The books grow.”

“The pressure grows.”

“And sometimes…”

“You begin to wonder if you’re falling behind.”

“One student felt exactly the same.”

Fade to black.

This is the level of detail Replit needs. If you give only one paragraph, it fills the gaps with simple cartoon graphics. If you give shot-by-shot directions like this, it has a much better chance of producing something cinematic.
