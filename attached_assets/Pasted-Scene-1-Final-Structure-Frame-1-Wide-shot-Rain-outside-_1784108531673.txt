Scene 1 (Final Structure)

Frame 1

Wide shot.

Rain outside.

Founder alone at desk.

Gray’s Anatomy, Guyton, Harper scattered.

Desk lamp is the only light source.

Clock shows 2:17 AM.

⸻

Frame 2

Medium shot.

Founder reading.

Eyes tired.

Hand holding pen.

Several crumpled papers nearby.

⸻

Frame 3

Over-shoulder shot.

Notebook page.

Huge checklist.

Only 2–3 topics ticked.

Many chapters remaining.

Examples:

✓ Upper Limb

✓ Thorax

✗ Abdomen

✗ Neuroanatomy

✗ CVS

✗ Respiration

✗ Endocrine

Frame 4

Extreme close-up.

Eyes blinking slowly.

Rain reflection visible in window.

No tears.

Just exhaustion.

⸻

Frame 5 (Important)

Founder looks toward window.

City lights outside.

Silence.

Voice-over:

“The syllabus seemed endless.”

Fade to black.

⸻

Scene 2 — The Decision

This is where the other two friends appear.

Hollywood films always have an “inciting incident.”

The founder realizes:

“Maybe the problem isn’t me.
Maybe every MBBS student is struggling.”

⸻

Shot 1

College canteen.

Afternoon.

Three friends sitting together.

Books everywhere.

Tired expressions.

No one talking.

⸻

Shot 2

One friend throws notebook on table.

Says:

“I can’t find proper notes.”

⸻

Shot 3

Second friend scrolling WhatsApp.

Hundreds of PDF groups.

Can’t find needed material.

Says:

“Everything is scattered.”

⸻

Shot 4

Founder listening silently.

Thinking.

No dialogue.

Only ambient sounds.

⸻

Shot 5

Close-up.

Founder opens notebook.

Writes one line:

Mission Distinction

for the first time.

⸻

Shot 6

Extreme close-up of pen writing “MD”.

Dramatic music begins.

Voice-over:

“What if everything students needed was in one place?”

⸻

Scene 2 Ending

Three friends look at notebook.

Nobody smiles.

Nobody celebrates.

Just determination.

Cut to black.

Text:

“An idea was born.”

⸻

This structure feels much more like a Netflix/Hollywood startup-origin film: Problem → Frustration → Realization → Idea, instead of immediately jumping into coding and success.
