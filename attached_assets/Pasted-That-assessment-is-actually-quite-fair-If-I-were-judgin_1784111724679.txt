Scene 3 Is Where The Film Can Become Great

Most startup films jump:

Idea → App

That’s boring.

The audience must earn the success.

Scene 3 should be:

The Collection

Three students start gathering resources.

Not coding.

Not launching.

Just collecting.

* Asking seniors
* Searching old drives
* Organizing PDFs
* Visiting library
* Taking photos of notes
* Naming folders

Thousands of tiny tasks.

Montage.

Days pass.

Weeks pass.

This is where viewers begin respecting the founders.
