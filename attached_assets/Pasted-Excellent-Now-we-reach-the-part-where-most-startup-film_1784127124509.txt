Excellent. Now we reach the part where most startup films fail.

Most films jump:

Idea → App → Success

But Oscar-level storytelling needs:

Idea → Work → Sacrifice → Failure → Persistence → Success

So the next scene should not be coding.

It should be the hardest and most relatable part:

🎬 SCENE 4 — THE COLLECTION

Theme: “Building began long before the app.”

Duration

75–90 seconds

Emotion Arc

Excitement → Reality → Exhaustion → Commitment

Visual Tone

Warm afternoons, late evenings, hostel corridors, libraries.

The audience should feel:

“These guys are doing real work.”

⸻

Camera

Top-down shot

Slow push-in

Why This Scene Matters

This is where the audience starts respecting the founders.

Before:

“Three students had an idea.”

After Scene 4:

“Three students worked for it.”

That’s a huge difference.

And dramatically, it’s essential because Scene 5 will introduce the first public release of PDFs/resources, where students begin using what the team created. That’s where the mission starts leaving the room and entering the real world. 🎬🍿
